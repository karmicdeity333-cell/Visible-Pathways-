About Visible Pathways

Visible Pathways was developed as part of a Phi Theta Kappa Honors in Action project at Pasco-Hernando State College. Through research and conversations with community organizations, our team explored how educational opportunities and support systems can be made more visible and accessible to individuals experiencing crisis or instability. Visible Pathways was created to help connect individuals with educational opportunities, support services, and community resources through a clear and easy-to-navigate platform.
