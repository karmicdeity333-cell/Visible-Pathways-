Transportation Can Open Doors

Reliable transportation can help people access education, employment, healthcare, and community resources.

Many public transit systems offer reduced-fare programs or even free monthly passes for eligible individuals, including college students, older adults, veterans, and individuals with disabilities. Eligibility requirements vary by location.

The examples below highlight several transportation resources available in Florida. However, many transit systems throughout the United States offer similar transportation assistance programs. We encourage visitors to explore transportation options in their local area to learn about available reduced-fare opportunities.
